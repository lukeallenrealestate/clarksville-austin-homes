## 8. Reviews, the biggest lever

Google ranks and recommends agents it sees reviewed, and reviews that mention
"Clarksville" tie you to the neighborhood in Google's eyes. Ask every happy
client, and nudge them to name the neighborhood.

**Get your review link:** in your Business Profile, tap "Ask for reviews" (or
"Get more reviews"). Copy the short link; it looks like
`https://g.page/r/XXXXXXXX/review`. Paste that link into the messages below.

**Text message (fastest):**
Hi [First name], it was a real pleasure helping you with your home in Clarksville.
If you have a minute, a short Google review about your experience, especially
anything specific about Clarksville or the neighborhood, would mean a lot and
helps others find me. Here's the link: [YOUR REVIEW LINK]. Thank you! - Luke

**Email:**
Subject: A quick favor?
Hi [First name],
It was a genuine pleasure helping you [buy / sell] in Clarksville. If you have a
couple of minutes, I'd be grateful for a short Google review. Mentioning
something specific, the neighborhood, a Clarksville block, what the process was
like, is the most helpful of all.
Here's the link: [YOUR REVIEW LINK]
Thank you, it makes a real difference for a neighborhood specialist like me.
Luke Allen, REALTOR, TREC #788149

**Reply to every review you receive** (Google rewards owner responses). When a
review mentions Clarksville, thank them and use the word "Clarksville" in your
reply.
