# Clarksville Austin Homes: Lead-Generation Outreach Kit

The site is built. 83 pages, deep content, beautiful design, lead capture wired to
your CRM and inbox. What it needs now is not more pages, it is **traffic and
authority**. This kit is the paint-by-numbers version of getting both. Work it
top to bottom; most of it is one-time or a light weekly habit.

Priority pages to point everything at:
- Sellers: `https://www.clarksvilleaustinhomes.com/clarksville-listing-agent`
- Buyers: `https://www.clarksvilleaustinhomes.com/buy-in-clarksville`
- Home: `https://www.clarksvilleaustinhomes.com/`

---

## Step 1 - Get indexed (this week, ~30 min)

Nothing ranks until Google crawls it. In Google Search Console:
- [ ] URL Inspection -> Request Indexing on, in order: home, `/clarksville-listing-agent`,
      `/buy-in-clarksville`, `/clarksville-homes-for-sale`, `/clarksville-realtor`,
      `/why-clarksville`, `/clarksville-market-report`, then the insights and showpieces.
      (~10/day; spread over the week.)
- [ ] Sitemaps -> confirm `sitemap.xml` shows "Success."
- [ ] Bing Webmaster Tools -> "Import from Google Search Console" (feeds Copilot + AI).

## Step 2 - Google Business Profile (highest-ROI, do this week)

"Clarksville realtor" shows a map pack ranked on your GBP, and it moves faster than
organic. You already have a 5-star profile.
- [ ] Category: **Real estate agent**. Service areas: Clarksville, 78703, Old West
      Austin, Tarrytown, Old Enfield, Pemberton Heights, Bryker Woods.
- [ ] Website field -> `https://www.clarksvilleaustinhomes.com/clarksville-listing-agent`
      (or home). Add your phone exactly as on the site.
- [ ] Add 10+ photos (neighborhood, listings, headshot). Post weekly (a listing, a
      market stat, an insights article, "just closed in 78703").
- [ ] **Reviews are the #1 map-pack factor.** Use the template in Step 5 after every
      closing, and ask reviewers to mention "Clarksville."

## Step 3 - Citations (one-time, ~1 hour)

Put your name, address, phone (NAP) and the website on every profile. Consistency
matters. These are expected, not "link schemes."
- [ ] Zillow agent profile (website field -> your site)
- [ ] Realtor.com, Homes.com agent profiles
- [ ] Bing Places, Apple Business Connect
- [ ] Yelp (Real Estate Agents), Nextdoor business page
- [ ] Your brokerage page (Austin Marketing + Development Group) -> link your site
- [ ] Every social bio: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, X
- [ ] Your email signature -> link the site

## Step 4 - Backlinks (the real ranking lever, ongoing)

Aim for 1-2 quality, relevant links a month. Local relevance beats volume. Targets,
roughly easiest first, each with the angle and a template below.

### A. Neighborhood + community orgs (highest value)
- **Old West Austin Neighborhood Association (OWANA)** - sponsor, join, or ask to be
  listed as a neighborhood real estate resource. Pitch the free history/landmarks
  content, not a sales page.
- **Clarksville Community Development Corporation (CDC)** - partnership / resource
  listing; your history content genuinely supports their mission.

### B. Partner businesses (reciprocal, easy)
- The **West Lynn corridor** businesses you already frequent or work with (a coffee
  shop, a shop, a service). Offer a "neighborhood partners" link exchange.
- Your mortgage lender, title company, inspector, stager, contractors -> partner links
  both ways.

### C. Local press + features (slow, powerful)
- **Austin Monthly, Austin American-Statesman, Community Impact, Towers.net,
  CultureMap Austin.** Your best hook is **The Clarksville development** (you are on
  the sales team, and Austin Monthly already covered it) and your primary-source
  neighborhood history. Pitch yourself as the go-to Clarksville expert source.
- **HARO / Qwoted / Help a B2B Writer** - answer reporter queries about Austin real
  estate, historic neighborhoods, off-market sales. Every published answer = a link.

### D. Content-driven links
- Your history, landmarks, and home-styles pages are genuinely link-worthy references.
  When anyone writes about Clarksville, yours is the best free resource - make sure
  they can find it (which is what the outreach below does).

## Step 6 - The weekly 30-minute routine

- **Mon:** 1 GBP post (link a page). Request-index any new/updated page in GSC.
- **Wed:** Send 2 outreach emails from Step 4/5. Answer 1 HARO/Qwoted query.
- **Fri:** Ask the week's closed/active clients for a Google review.
- **Monthly:** Check GSC Performance (which queries/pages are gaining?) and deepen
  whatever is close to page one.

## What NOT to do
- Don't buy links or use link networks. Don't spin up more thin pages (it now hurts).
- Don't fake reviews (TREC + Google violation). Real ones mentioning Clarksville are gold.
- Don't judge results before ~90 days. This compounds; give it a season.
